# RAED Dynamics brand reference

The app header uses the official RAED wordmark and company name. The official homepage identifies the brand as RAED Dynamics; the site footer uses the formal name RAED Dynamics Inc. [1] The logo asset was taken from the official site's `/assets/logo-BR0I9Hep.png` image and stored locally as `src/raedbots/web/raed-dynamics-logo.png` so the app does not depend on a third-party request at runtime. [2]

The official site stylesheet uses `#EE3124` as a prominent red brand color. The app retains its dark engineering-workspace palette and uses the red only as a restrained accent in the company lockup. [3]
